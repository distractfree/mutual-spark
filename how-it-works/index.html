How It Works

A private path to the same next step.

Mutual Spark compares independent Connection Requests. It does not expose one person’s request to the other.

1

Verify your phone

Your verified number identifies your account and receives transactional updates.

2

Choose your request

Select Ask Out, Go for Another Date, or Change Relationship.

3

Enter their number

Your request stays private. We do not reveal whether that number is registered.

4

Wait for an update

A match requires the other person to independently submit the same request type for you.

Mutual Spark does not invite unregistered targets during this beta.
